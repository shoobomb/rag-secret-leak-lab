# Internal FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, ask in `#ask-people-ops`.

## Pay & Benefits

**When am I paid?**
On the 15th and last business day of each month.

**How do I enroll in benefits?**
You have 30 days from your start date. Open the HR portal and select *Benefits > Enroll*.

**Does Lumenstack match 401(k) contributions?**
Yes, up to 4% of salary, vested immediately.

## Time Off

**How much PTO do I get?**
20 days per year, plus 10 company holidays. Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days.

**How do I request time off?**
Submit the request in the HR portal and notify your manager. Requests of 5+ days should be made at least two weeks ahead.

## Tools & Access

**I'm locked out of my account. What do I do?**
Post in `#it-help` or use the self-service reset at the IT portal.

**Can I install my own software?**
Approved software is in the Self Service app. For anything else, file an IT request.

## Expenses

**How do I get reimbursed?**
Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days. Meals while traveling are covered up to $75/day.

## Other

**Where is the org chart?**
On the wiki under *Company > People*.

**Who do I contact to report a concern?**
Your manager, People Ops, or the anonymous Ethics Line at ethics@lumenstack.example.
