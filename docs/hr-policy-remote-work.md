# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Company:** Lumenstack Inc. (fictional)
**Owner:** People Operations
**Effective:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.3

## Purpose
This policy sets expectations for where and how Lumenstack employees work, so teams can collaborate well while keeping flexibility.

## Eligibility
All full-time and part-time employees are eligible after completing their 30-day onboarding period. Roles that require on-site presence (e.g., hardware lab, facilities) are listed in the job description.

## Work Arrangements
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Hybrid** (default) | In office Tuesday and Thursday; remote otherwise |
| **Remote** | Fully remote with manager and People Ops approval |
| **On-site** | Required for roles tied to physical equipment |

## Core Hours
All employees should be reachable between **10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone**. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible if team commitments are met.

## Equipment & Stipend
- One laptop and one monitor are provided at hire.
- A **$600 one-time home office stipend** is available in the first 90 days.
- A **$50/month internet reimbursement** applies to remote employees.

## Security Requirements
1. Use the company VPN on any network that is not your home network.
2. Lock your screen when stepping away.
3. Never store company data on personal devices or unapproved cloud storage.

## Working From Abroad
Up to **20 working days per calendar year** may be worked from another country with prior approval. Longer stays may have tax and legal implications and require review by Legal.

## Exceptions & Questions
Submit exception requests through the HR portal. Contact people-ops@lumenstack.example with questions.
