# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Lumenstack! This guide covers your first 30 days.

## Before Day 1
- [ ] Sign your offer paperwork in the HR portal
- [ ] Confirm your laptop shipping address
- [ ] Check your inbox for your Day 1 schedule

## Week 1: Get Set Up
| Day | What happens |
|-----|--------------|
| Mon | Welcome session, laptop setup, meet your buddy |
| Tue | Security training, accounts and access review |
| Wed | Team intro and tour of the product |
| Thu | Shadow a teammate; first 1:1 with your manager |
| Fri | Team lunch and Week 1 check-in |

## Your Accounts
You will be given access to:
- **Email & Calendar** (Workspace)
- **Slack**: join `#general`, `#announcements`, and your team channel
- **Wiki**: handbook, org chart, and team docs
- **Ticketing**: Jira (engineering) or Helpdesk (everyone else)

## Weeks 2–4: Ramp Up
1. **Week 2:** Complete role-specific training and meet key partners.
2. **Week 3:** Take on a small starter project with your buddy's support.
3. **Week 4:** Hold a 30-day review with your manager to set 90-day goals.

## Who to Ask
| Topic | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Laptop or access problems | #it-help |
| Pay, benefits, time off | people-ops@lumenstack.example |
| Anything else | Your onboarding buddy |

## Tips
- Ask questions early and often; nobody expects you to know everything.
- Write down what confuses you and suggest doc fixes. Fresh eyes are valuable.
